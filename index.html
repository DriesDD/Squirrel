Twenty-Five Rooms

A 5 × 5 maze of rooms. You start in the centre. One gate on the outer wall leads out. Every room is split up by puzzles and small mazes. Some puzzles only work in one direction, but there is always a way back.

    Controls

    Move with ← ↑ → ↓. Walk into crates and doors to push them. Z undoes a push, R resets the room and puts you back where you came in.

    Pieces

    • Crate. Push from any side, up to five in a row. Four or five are slow.
    • Sliding door. Moves only along its rail, pushed from either end.
    • Gapped wall. Only doors on a rail pass through.
    • Ice. You, crates, doors and snowballs keep sliding until they leave the ice or hit something.
    • Snow. Push it to roll a snowball; rolling over snow or into another ball makes it bigger.
    • Snowball. Small ones break when they hit something, sizes 3 and 4 act like crates, size 5 is a wall of snow.
